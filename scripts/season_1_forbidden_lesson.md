# 🎬 FORBIDDEN LESSON
### A SnackDrama Original Series — Season 1
**Format:** Vertical Short-Form Drama | 15 Episodes | 60–90 sec each

---

## 🧑‍🎭 CHARACTER PROFILES

---

### KARIM
- **Age:** 19
- **Look:** Tall, lean, dark olive skin, sharp jaw, intense dark eyes that miss nothing. Worn but neatly pressed clothes — a scholarship kid who irons everything he owns.
- **Personality:** Quietly confident, dangerously intelligent, dry humour. Underneath: deeply wounded by poverty and a broken home. He doesn't beg — he earns.
- **Motivation:** Prove that genius doesn't care where you were born.

---

### LEILA
- **Age:** 28
- **Look:** Elegant, naturally beautiful, always professional — tailored blazers, silk blouses, hair pinned up with one strand always falling loose. Her eyes are warm but carry the weight of choices made too early.
- **Personality:** Brilliant, composed in public, silently suffocating inside her perfect life. Passionate about literature and truth — both dangerous things.
- **Motivation:** She chose safety over love once. She is terrified of doing it again.

---

### FARES
- **Age:** 35
- **Look:** Handsome in a cold, curated way. Expensive watch, tailored suit, the kind of man who smiles with only his mouth.
- **Personality:** Controlling, possessive, charming to the world and dismissive in private. He sees Leila as an acquisition, not a partner.
- **Motivation:** Own everything — including her.

---

### NADIA
- **Age:** 19
- **Look:** Bright, bubbly, pretty in a girl-next-door way. Always near Karim. Always watching.
- **Personality:** Sweet on the surface, quietly desperate underneath. Her love for Karim curdles into obsession when she senses she's losing him.
- **Motivation:** She was supposed to be his future. Then Leila walked in.

---

## 🔓 EPISODE 1 — "First Day"
**[FREE]**

---

**SCENE SETTING:**
> University lecture hall. Morning. Sunlight cuts through tall windows in sharp golden beams. The room hums with first-day energy — students chattering, phones out. The chalk board reads: *CONTEMPORARY LITERATURE — DR. LEILA MANSOUR.*

---

**[ESTABLISHING SHOT — wide, low angle on the door]**

*The door opens. LEILA walks in. The room goes quiet.*

**[SLOW MOTION — her heels on marble floor, students turning one by one]**

**LEILA:** *(sets her bag down, looks up, meets the room)*
"I don't take attendance. I don't chase you. But if you walk out of this class — don't come back."

**[TENSE]** *Murmurs. A few nervous laughs.*

*In the back row — KARIM sits alone. No phone. No laptop. Just a worn notebook and a pen. He studies her like she's a puzzle.*

**KARIM:** *(low, to himself)*
"Interesting."

**[ROMANTIC — soft focus on his eyes as they track her]**

**LEILA:** *(scanning the room — her gaze lands on him. She pauses. Barely.)*
"We'll begin with a question. What is the most dangerous thing a human being can feel?"

*Silence. Students look at each other.*

**KARIM:** *(without raising his hand, calm and clear)*
"Hope."

**[SHOCK — LEILA freezes for a half second. Their eyes lock. Time slows.]**

**LEILA:** *(almost a whisper)*
"...Correct."

**[SMASH CUT — bell rings. Episode ends on their locked gaze.]**

---

**🎨 SCENE VISUAL NOTES:**
> Key shot: Mid-close on LEILA's face — one shaft of morning light across her eyes — as she registers KARIM's answer. Background students soft-bokeh blurred. Colour grade: warm amber and ivory. Mood: *The moment before a storm.*
> **Midjourney prompt style:** *cinematic vertical portrait, elegant woman professor in lecture hall, golden morning light, emotional eye contact, soft bokeh background, warm amber tones, film grain*

## 🔓 EPISODE 2 — "After Class"
**[FREE]**

---

**SCENE SETTING:**
> The lecture hall empties. Late afternoon. The golden light has shifted — now it's amber and low, cutting long shadows across empty seats. LEILA erases the board. Footsteps. She doesn't turn.

---

**[WIDE — the empty hall, KARIM walking toward her, hands in pockets]**

**LEILA:** *(still erasing, not looking)*

**KARIM:**
"I had a follow-up question."

**LEILA:** *(turns, arms crossed)*
"Office hours are Thursdays."

**KARIM:** *(small smile)*
"Is that a rule or a preference?"

**[TENSE — she stares at him. He doesn't flinch.]**

**LEILA:**

**LEILA:** *(quietly, controlled)*
"You're correct."

**KARIM:**
"I know."

**[POWER MOMENT — he holds her gaze, unhurried. She is unreadable. He turns to leave.]**

**KARIM:** *(over his shoulder)*
"I'll see you Thursday, Dr. Mansour."

*She watches him go. Her hand grips the notebook tighter than she intends.*

**[SMASH CUT — her phone buzzes. Screen: ❤️ FARES. She doesn't pick up.]**

---

**🎨 SCENE VISUAL NOTES:**
